Privacy policy

Effective 23 November 2026

LifeMeter is built so that your answers never leave your phone. There is no account, no LifeMeter server, no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter is stored only on your device, in the app’s private storage:

  • your birthday, country and sex, and your name if you give one;
  • your answers to the optional health questions;
  • your one thing, bucket list, week memories and the people you add;
  • your settings, such as theme and widget options.

The home-screen widgets read the same on-device data. To erase it all, use Settings → Start over, or uninstall the app.

What leaves your phone

  • The quote of the day. Once a day the app downloads a quote from ZenQuotes. The request contains nothing about you. Like any website, ZenQuotes can see your device’s IP address when it answers.
  • Things you share. Share cards are made on your phone and only go where you send them through your phone’s share sheet. Your name appears on a card only if you turn it on.
  • Links. Links in the app (such as this page) open in your browser.

Nothing else is sent anywhere.

Permissions

  • Internet: only for the quote of the day.
  • Set wallpaper: used only when you choose “Set as lock screen”.
  • Run at startup, keep awake, network state and foreground service: added automatically by Android’s background-work library, which the home-screen widget library includes. LifeMeter uses them only to keep its widgets up to date.

Children

LifeMeter is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
