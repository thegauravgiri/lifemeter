Help

Answers to common questions, and how to reach me.

How is my number calculated?

From life expectancy for your country and sex, nudged by any optional health answers. See How it works.

Why is my estimate different from another app’s?

Apps use different data and adjustments. LifeMeter uses World Bank and UN data and a few well-studied habits, and shows a range rather than a date. Every estimate like this is approximate.

How do I add or resize the widgets?

In the app, open Settings → Widgets and lock screen and tap the widget you want, or long-press your home screen and choose Widgets → LifeMeter. To resize, long-press a widget and drag its handles. The countdown and life-in-weeks widgets shrink to two cells wide, so they fit side by side.

How do I change what the countdown widget shows?

Settings → Countdown widget shows: a slideshow, or one of Life Clock, years, weeks, days, hours or a live seconds countdown.

How do I change my details or start over?

Everything you entered can be edited in Settings. Start over erases all your data from the phone.

Is my data backed up or shared?

No. It stays on your phone. See the privacy policy.
