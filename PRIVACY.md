# LifeMeter Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 23 November 2026
**App:** LifeMeter (Android package `np.com.gauravgiri.lifemeter`)
**Developer:** Gaurav Giri — gaurovgiri@gmail.com
**Website:** https://lifemeter.gauravgiri.com.np

LifeMeter is built so that your answers stay on your phone. There is no account and no LifeMeter server. The app has no advertising and no analytics or tracking, and the developer does not collect, receive, store or sell any personal data.

## 1. Information you enter, and where it is kept

Everything you enter is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage:

- your birthday, country and sex, and your name if you choose to give one;
- your answers to the optional health questions (smoking, activity, alcohol, body weight, diet);
- your "one thing", your bucket list, notes you add to weeks, and the people you add (a name or label, a birth year and how often you see them);
- your settings, such as the theme and the widget options.

The app uses this information only to calculate and show your estimate, your Life Clock and your widgets. It is never sent to the developer or anyone else.

**Deleting your data:** open **Settings → Start over** in the app to erase everything, or uninstall the app. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, there is nothing for the developer to delete on your behalf.

## 2. What leaves your device

- **Quote of the day.** Once a day the app downloads a quote from [ZenQuotes](https://zenquotes.io/) (zenquotes.io). The request contains no information about you. As with any internet request, ZenQuotes can see your device's IP address when it answers; LifeMeter does not send it anything else. See ZenQuotes' own terms for how they handle requests.
- **Things you choose to share.** Share cards and images are created on your device and only go where you send them using your phone's share sheet. Your name appears on a card only if you turn that option on.
- **Links.** Links in the app (such as to this policy) open in your web browser.

Nothing else is sent anywhere.

## 3. Permissions

- **Internet:** used only to download the quote of the day.
- **Set wallpaper:** used only when you choose **Set as lock screen**.
- **Run at startup, keep awake, network state and foreground service:** added automatically by Android's background-work library, which the home-screen widget library includes. LifeMeter uses them only to keep its widgets up to date. They do not collect any data.

## 4. Third parties

LifeMeter contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software. The only third-party service it contacts is ZenQuotes, as described above.

## 5. Health information

The optional health answers are used only on your device to adjust the estimate. They are never transmitted. LifeMeter gives a population-based estimate; it is not a prediction and not medical advice.

## 6. Children

LifeMeter is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## 7. Security

Your information stays in the app's private storage on your device, protected by Android's app sandbox. Because the developer never receives your data, there is no server copy that could be exposed.

## 8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address with a new effective date.
