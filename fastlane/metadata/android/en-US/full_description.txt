What time is it in your life?

If your life were a single day, what time would it be right now? If you've lived 45% of your expected years, it's 10:54 AM. Most of the day is still ahead, but the morning is gone, and it isn't coming back.

LifeMeter turns the time you have left into something you can see: a clock, a grid of weeks, and the moments that matter. Not to scare you. To wake you up, gently, every time you look at your phone.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

YOUR LIFE CLOCK
Your life mapped onto 24 hours. Watch it switch between the Life Clock and the weeks, days, hours and live seconds you have left, with a progress bar of the life you've already lived.

YOUR LIFE IN WEEKS
Every week of your life as one small square, one row per year. The ones behind you are filled in. The ones ahead are blank. This week is pink. Add a memory to any week and watch your life fill with colour.

THE TAIL END
How many summers do you have left? Weekends? Full moons? Books, at five a year? Seeing it as a number changes how you spend the next one.

YOUR PEOPLE
"About 50 more visits with Mum." Add the people you love and see how much time together is left, counted kindly, with a nudge to plan the next visit.

YOUR ONE THING, AND A BUCKET LIST
Name the one thing you'd regret not doing and keep it beside your count. Add everything else to a bucket list, and tick it off by living it.

KEEP IT IN SIGHT
• Home-screen widgets: a countdown (as a slideshow or the unit you choose), your life in weeks, and the Life Clock. They fit side by side.
• Your life grid as your lock-screen wallpaper.
• A short motivational quote every day.
• Milestones worth celebrating, like 10,000 days old.

SHARE YOUR TIME
One tap turns your Life Clock or your weeks into a story-ready card: "What time is it in your life?" Ask a friend.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

HONEST NUMBERS
Your estimate starts from life expectancy for your country and sex: World Bank / UN data for 217 countries. Five optional, research-backed questions (smoking, activity, alcohol, weight, diet) refine it. You see a range, never a date, and the full method and sources are in the app.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No ads. No tracking. Your answers stay on your phone, and you can erase everything from Settings.

Set up in 30 seconds: your birthday, your country, and you'll see your time.

Inspired by Tim Urban's "Your Life in Weeks" and Oliver Burkeman's "Four Thousand Weeks". A life calendar and memento mori for people who'd rather live than worry.

LifeMeter shows an estimate from population data. It is not a prediction and not medical advice.
